## **Course Title: The AI-Native Professional: Building Your Digital Factory**

**The Delivery Loop:** **Show** (Instructor high-energy demo)  **Build** (Students apply R-C-T-C to a project)  **Refine** (Group critique of "Managerial Logic").

---

### **Day 1: Architecting the Brain**

**Goal:** Transition from treating AI as a search engine to treating it as a permanent member of your operations team.

#### **Session 1: The AI-Native Mindset (09:00 – 09:45)**

*   **The Paradigm Shift:** Moving from "Question & Answer" (Google) to "Thinking Partner" (LLM).
*   **The 3 Traps of AI:** Treating it like Search, Treating it like a Person, Over-trusting.
*   **Safety First:** The "Newspaper Test." Never upload what you wouldn't want on the front page.
*   **Data Hygiene:** Applying **Data Sanitization** (anonymizing names/secrets) before any upload.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz: Identify the "Safe vs. Risky" prompt.

#### **Session 2: The First Interaction – The Research Stack (10:00 – 10:45)**

*   **The New Workflow:** Start with **context**, not a blank page.
*   **The Trust Layer:** Upload sources (PDFs, docs) to create a grounded research assistant that **cites its work** (The Antidote to Hallucinations).
*   **Tools in Action:** **NotebookLM** (Google) / **Deep Research** (Perplexity/ChatGPT) / **Project Knowledge** (Claude).
*   **Activity:** "The 10-Hour Research Task in 10 Minutes." Students upload a complex document (e.g., a competitor report or industry whitepaper) and generate a 1-page executive summary and an "Audio Overview" (if using NotebookLM).

#### **Session 3: The Science of Delegation (R-C-T-C) (11:00 – 12:30)**

*   **The Framework:** Master the **Role-Context-Task-Constraint** (R-C-T-C) framework to ensure high-quality "first-shot" results.
*   **Instructor Demo:** Building a **Client Relationship Assistant** live.
    *   *Scenario:* Taking messy notes from a call with "Lisa" where a deal was almost lost due to vague follow-up.
    *   *Solution:* Creating a structured prompt that parses messy notes into: Context, Key Points, Decisions, Action Items (Owner/Deadline).
*   **Student Activity:** The "Project Lead" Simulation (Part 1).
    *   *Scenario:* You are a Project Lead. You just finished a research phase (Session 2).
    *   *Task:* Write an R-C-T-C prompt to draft a **Client Update Email** summarizing your research findings for a stakeholder.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on spotting the missing element in an R-C-T-C prompt.

#### **Session 4: Building "Specialized Brains" (Context) (13:30 – 15:00)**

*   **Global vs. Local Context:** Move from the "Chat Window" (short-term memory) to a **Gem** (Gemini) or **Project/GPT** (Claude/ChatGPT) (long-term memory including personal memory and preferences).
*   **The "Digital Twin" Concept:** How to build a persistent assistant that knows your style and rules.
*   **Practical Application:** Convert the "Client Relationship Assistant" or "Project Lead" prompt from Session 3 into a reusable **Gem**.
    *   *Input:* Messy notes/emails.
    *   *Output:* Perfectly formatted updates, every time.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on when to use a Chat vs. a Gem.

#### **Session 5: The Assembly Line (SOPs & Workflows) (15:15 – 16:30)**

*   **The Reality:** "Garbage In, Garbage Out." We all know that vague inputs lead to messy outputs.

*   **The Solution:** **Markdown SOPs**. Teaching the AI a step-by-step Standard Operating Procedure.
*   **Student Activity:** The "Expense Report" Nightmare.
    *   *Scenario:* It's the end of the month. You have a crumpled photo of a dinner receipt, a forwarded airline confirmation email, and a sporadic list of taxi rides in WhatsApp.
    *   *Task:* Create an SOP that takes these **3 Chaotic Inputs** and consolidates them into a single **clean CSV Table** (Date | Category | Amount | Vendor) ready for Session 6.
    *   **The Hook:** Explicitly state that this clean financial data is the *fuel* for the "Data Factory" in Session 6.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on SOP logic.

---

### **Day 2: Advanced Intelligence & Executive Output**

**Goal:** Master data-driven storytelling, and personal productivity.

#### **Session 6: The Data Factory (Data Enrichment) (09:00 – 10:45)**

*   **From Chaos to Order:** Taking the "Financial Data" from Session 5 and structuring it for deep analysis.
*   **The Workflow Pattern:** "The Analyst" (Upload Data -> Query -> Visualize).
*   **Data Enrichment (The 3 Layers):** 
    1.  **The "Smart Categorizer":** AI auto-tags vague expenses (e.g., "Starbucks" -> "Meals & Entertainment", "Delta" -> "Travel").
    2.  **The "Merchant Cleaner":** Normalizing messy names (e.g., "Uber*Trip-2847" -> "Uber").
    3.  **The "Policy Police":** Anomalies detection (e.g., Flagging "Saturday night dinner" or "Duplicate Amount").
*   **Activity:** Preparing the **"Expense Analysis Dashboard"** & **"Compliance Report"** for executive review.

#### **Session 7: Interviewing Your Data (11:00 – 12:45)**

*   **Conversational Analysis:** Skip complex formulas (VLOOKUP/Pivot Tables).
*   **The "Interview":** Ask your data questions like a colleague:
    *   "What is our fastest-growing complaint category?"
    *   "Summarize the sales trends for Q3 vs Q2."
*   **Financial Scenarios:** Use AI to model "What If" budget scenarios based on the enriched data.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on conversational command structures.

#### **Session 8: Personal Productivity Systems (13:45 – 15:30)**

*   **Custom Instructions:** Set your "Default Persona" (Role, Tone, Format) so the AI always sounds like *you* (not a robot).
*   **The Prompt Library:** Build a personal "Recipe Book" in Notion or a spreadsheet for recurring tasks.
    *   *Examples:* Job Interview Prep, Meeting Summaries, Difficult Conversation Scripts.
*   **Habit Building:** Plan how to make using AI a "natural reflex" for your identified bottlenecks.
*   **Activity:** Identify one "Personal Bottleneck" (e.g., email drafting) and build a quick solution for it.

#### **Session 9: The Finale & ROI (15:45 – 17:30) (Optional/Flexible)**

*   **The Presentation Stack:** Use **Deep Research** → **Structure** (Gemini/Claude) → **Design** (Slides/Gamma) to build the final pitch.
*   **ROI Calculation:** Use the formula **(Task Frequency x Duration x % AI Efficiency)** to calculate exactly how many hours per week you have "won back."
*   **Showcase:** 3-minute presentation on "The one process I will never do manually again."
*   **Micro-Check:** Final Quiz on course ROI and key takeaways.
