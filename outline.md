## **Course Title: The AI-Native Professional: Building Your Digital Factory**

**The Delivery Loop:** **Show** (Instructor high-energy demo)  **Build** (Students apply R-C-T-C to a project)  **Refine** (Group critique of "Managerial Logic").

---

### **Day 1: Architecting the Brain**

**Goal:** Transition from treating AI as a search engine to treating it as a permanent member of your operations team.

#### **Session 1: The AI-Native Mindset & Safety (09:00 – 10:30)**

* **The Hook:** Contrast a "lazy" prompt (e.g., "Write a social media post") with an "AI-Native" workflow that produces elite results instantly.
* **Safety First:** The "Newspaper Test." Never upload what you wouldn't want on the front page. Apply **Data Sanitization** (anonymizing names/secrets) before any upload.

* **Capabilities Showcase:** Real-world examples of AI usage and tool overview.

* **The AI Mind:** Understanding how LLMs think and managing "Hallucination".

* **The Trust Layer (NotebookLM):** Upload sources (PDFs, docs) to create a grounded research assistant that **cites its work** (The Antidote to Hallucinations).
* **The Creative Layer:** Use **NotebookLM** (Audio Overviews) or **Multimodal Tools** (Firefly/Canva) to brainstorm ideas and generate visual assets.


* **When to Use AI:** Establish the "Yes/No" framework. Use AI for **repetitive, data-intensive, or brainstorming** tasks. Use caution for **high-stakes** or **deeply human** situations.
* **Activity:** Identify your "Personal Bottleneck" (e.g., email drafting or meeting notes) to solve during the course.
* **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz to check understanding of "AI Mindset" and Safety.

#### **Session 2: The Science of Delegation (R-C-T-C) (10:45 – 12:30)**

* **The Framework:** Master the **Role-Context-Task-Constraint** (R-C-T-C) framework to ensure high-quality "first-shot" results.
* **Overcoming the Blank Page:** Use AI to brainstorm ideas for new products or visualize futuristic spaces using image generation.
* **Instructor Demo:** Building a **Client Relationship Assistant** live from a **Medium article**.
* **Student Activity:** Write a manual R-C-T-C prompt to draft a complex email to a client.
* **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz to test R-C-T-C framework application.

#### **Session 3: Building "Specialized Brains" (13:30 – 15:00)**

* **Global vs. Local Context:** Move from the "Chat Window" (short-term memory) to a **Gem** or **Project** (long-term memory).

* **Practical Application:** Convert the previous session's project into a reusable Gem.

* **Personal Memory:** Introduction to User Memory/Custom Instructions and how to leverage them.


* **Student Activity:** Create a "Gem" for a repetitive task—like **Content Curation**.
* **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on Gem/Project context retention.

#### **Session 4: The Assembly Line (SOPs & Workflows) (15:15 – 17:00)**


Before they automate, students must feel the friction of the manual process (copy-pasting between chats):

Step 1: Paste a chaotic, consistency-breaking list of expenses (mixed dates, currencies, and formats) into the AI to force manual correction friction.

Step 2: Ask the AI to categorize them (Food, Transport, Entertainment).

Step 3: Manually copy that result and ask the AI to format it into a CSV table.

Step 4: Copy the CSV and paste it into Google Sheets.

* **The Playbook:** Convert that manual chain into a **Markdown SOP** and "install" it into your Gem/Project brain.
* **The Payoff:** Execute a complex 4-step workflow with a single "One-Sentence Delegation."
* **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on SOP creation logic.

---

### **Day 2: Advanced Intelligence & Executive Output**

**Goal:** Master data-driven storytelling, and personal productivity.

#### **Session 5: The Data Factory (Data Enrichment) (09:00 – 10:45)**

* **From Chaos to Order:** Taking the "Financial Data" from Session 4 and structuring it for deep analysis.
* **Data Enrichment:** Automatically tagging and categorizing, using another method inside sheets, entries before the "Interview" (Session 6).

* **Activity:** Preparing the **Financial Dashboard Dataset** for the executive review.
* **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on Data Enrichment principles.

#### **Session 6: Interviewing Your Data (11:00 – 12:45)**

* **Conversational Analysis:** Use **Gemini in Google Sheets** to skip complex formulas. Ask the data questions like: "What is our fastest-growing category?" or "Summarize the sales trends for Q3".
* **Financial Scenarios:** Use AI to model "What If" budget scenarios or generate a financial report from raw data.
* **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on conversational data analysis.

#### **Session 7: Personal Productivity Systems (13:45 – 15:30)**

* **Custom Instructions:** Set your "Default Persona" (Role, Tone, Format) so the AI always sounds like you.
* **The Prompt Library:** Build a personal "Recipe Book" in Notion or a spreadsheet for recurring tasks like **Job Interview Prep** or **Meeting Summaries**.
* **Habit Building:** Plan how to make using AI a "natural reflex" for your identified bottlenecks.
* **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on Productivity System design.

#### **Session 8: The Finale & ROI (15:45 – 17:30) (optional)**

* **The Presentation Stack:** Use **Deep Research** → **Gemini** (Structure) → **Google Slides** (Design) to build a store management pitch.
* **ROI Calculation:** Use the formula **(Task Frequency x Duration x % AI Efficiency)** to calculate exactly how many hours per week you have "won back."
* **Showcase:** 3-minute presentation on "The one process I will never do manually again."
* **Micro-Check:** Final Quiz on course ROI and key takeaways.
