## **Course Title: AI at Work: Build the Workflows That Win Back Your Week**

*A 2-day workshop to turn the work you repeat into reusable, measurable AI workflows.*

**The Delivery Loop:** **Show** (Instructor high-energy demo) → **Build** (Students apply a workflow to a real task from their backlog) → **Refine** (Group critique of the business logic).

**The Running Spine:** On Day 1 every participant writes an **Automation Backlog** (the recurring tasks that eat their week) and starts a simple **Time Log**. Every build session, they pull one task from that backlog, build a workflow for it, and record the before/after time. By the finale they total it into **hours won back per week** — the proof the course worked.

> See `prompts/automation_backlog_and_time_log.md` for the worksheet participants fill in throughout.

### **Day 1: Build Your First Workflows**

**Goal:** Stop treating AI as a search engine. Start treating it as a member of your operations team — and leave with reusable workflows, not one-off answers.

#### **Session 1: The AI-Native Mindset & Your Automation Backlog (09:00 – 09:45)**

*   **The Paradigm Shift:** Moving from "Question & Answer" (Google) to "Thinking Partner" (AI teammate).
*   **The 3 Traps of AI:** Treating it like Search, Treating it like a Person, Over-trusting.
*   **Where AI Pays Off:** The bottleneck test — repetitive, data-heavy, drafting, and predictable work first; high-stakes, deeply human, confidential, or originality-driven work with caution.
*   **Safety First:** The "Newspaper Test." Never upload what you wouldn't want on the front page. Apply **Data Sanitization** (anonymize names/secrets) before any upload.
*   **Build (Deliverable):** Each participant lists **3–5 recurring tasks** as their **Automation Backlog** and logs current time-per-task in the **Time Log**. This is the target list for the rest of the course.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz: Identify the "Safe vs. Risky" prompt.

#### **Session 2: Delegating to AI — The RICE Pattern (10:00 – 11:30)**

*   **The Reframe:** Stop chatting. Start delegating. You don't need a chatbot — you need a direct report.
*   **The Pattern:** Give AI what you'd give a new hire — **Role, Instructions, Context, Examples** (RICE) — so you get a usable result on the first pass.
*   **Instructor Demo:** Building a **Client Relationship Assistant** live.
    *   *Scenario:* Messy notes from a call with "Lisa" where a deal was almost lost to a vague follow-up.
    *   *Solution:* A structured delegation that turns the notes into: Summary, Decisions, Action Items (Owner/Deadline), Clarifications Needed.
*   **Build (from your backlog):** Pick a recurring "turn messy input into a clean output" task and write a RICE delegation for it. Log the time saved.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on spotting the missing element in a RICE delegation.

#### **Session 3: Reusable Workflows — Building Specialized Assistants (11:45 – 12:30)**

*   **The "Groundhog Day" Problem:** A great one-off delegation is forgotten the moment the chat closes. Productivity comes from **reuse**, not re-typing.
*   **Local vs. Global Context:** Move from the disposable "Chat Window" to a persistent assistant — a **Gem** (Gemini), **Project** (Claude), or **Custom GPT** (ChatGPT) that remembers your style, rules, and preferences.
*   **The "Digital Twin" Concept:** A specialist that already knows your handbook, tone, and format.
*   **Build (Workflow):** Convert the Session 2 delegation into a **reusable assistant**. Run new input through it and watch it produce the right output with no setup.
*   **The ROI:** ~5 minutes to set up once vs. minutes saved on every future run — log the recurring saving against your backlog item.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on when to use a Chat vs. a saved assistant.

#### **Session 4: Grounding AI in Truth — The Research Workflow (13:30 – 15:00)**

*   **The New Workflow:** Start with **context**, not a blank page. Stop reading 100 pages by hand.
*   **The Trust Layer:** Upload your sources (PDFs, docs) so the assistant answers **only** from them and **cites its work** — the antidote to hallucinations.
*   **Tools in Action:** **NotebookLM** (Google) / **Deep Research** (Perplexity/ChatGPT) / **Project Knowledge** (Claude).
*   **Build (from your backlog):** "The 10-Hour Research Task in 10 Minutes." Upload a complex document and produce a verified 1-page executive summary (and an Audio Overview if using NotebookLM). Log the time saved.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on choosing the right tool and the safety check before uploading.

#### **Session 5: Solving Hard Problems (15:15 – 16:30)**

*   **Why AI fumbles smart questions:** By default it answers fast and shallow. For complex tasks you have to make it slow down. Three moves do that:
*   **Move 1 — Think step by step** *(a.k.a. Chain of Thought)*: make the AI show its reasoning before answering, so it catches its own logic errors. Best for logic, math, scheduling.
*   **Move 2 — Debate it out** *(a.k.a. Tree of Thoughts)*: have the AI argue from several expert viewpoints, then synthesize. Best for strategy and judgment calls.
*   **Move 3 — Double-check** *(a.k.a. Self-Consistency)*: have the AI solve the same thing independently a few times and flag any disagreement. Best for numbers and data extraction.
*   **Build (from your backlog):** Take a recurring decision or calculation task and apply the matching move (e.g. build a constrained schedule with "think step by step").
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on matching the problem type to the right move.

### **Day 2: Executive Output & Personal Systems**

**Goal:** Turn your workflows into board-ready output, conversations with your data, and a personal system you'll actually keep using.

#### **Session 6: From Notes to Narrative (09:00 – 10:15)**

*   **The Problem:** "The Frankenstein Deck." Slides pasted from five files with no coherent story.
*   **The Solution:** **The Amazon Memo Method**. Narrative first, visuals second.
*   **The Workflow:** Use "think step by step" to pressure-test your argument before you ever open a slide tool.
    *   *Step 1: The Draft.* Write the 1-page narrative (the pitch).
    *   *Step 2: The Roast.* Ask the AI to act as a skeptical CFO and attack the logic.
    *   *Step 3: The Blueprint.* Convert the refined narrative → slide outline → visual descriptors.
*   **Build (from your backlog):** "The Strategic Pitch." Choose one — Remote Work Policy / Budget for a New Tool / a New Hire — and produce a roasted, board-ready blueprint.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on why we write before we design.

#### **Session 7: The Design Engine (10:30 – 12:00)**

*   **The Goal:** Speed & polish. Turn your Session 6 blueprint into a board-ready deck in minutes.
*   **Method A — The "One-Click" Wonder (Gamma):** Paste outline → generate deck.
*   **Method B — The "Corporate-Safe" Way (Word/Markdown → PowerPoint):** Outline → official template, zero manual formatting.
*   **Build:** "The Emergency Board Update." It's 11:50 AM; the board meets at 12:00. Build a 5-slide status deck from raw crisis notes in 10 minutes. Log the time saved.
*   **Outcome:** A professional, on-brand deck with no manual formatting.

#### **Session 8: Interviewing Your Data (13:00 – 14:30)**

*   **Conversational Analysis:** Skip VLOOKUP and pivot tables — ask your data questions like a colleague.
    *   "What is our fastest-growing expense category?"
    *   "Summarize the spending trends for Q3 vs Q2."
*   **Extract & Enrich:** Pull structured data from a receipt image (OCR), then reconcile it against the expense export.
*   **"What If" Scenarios:** Model budget changes on the enriched data.
*   **Build (from your backlog):** Run a real "interview" on `course_content/data/expenses_export.csv`. Log the time saved vs. building formulas.
*   **Micro-Check:** Live Quiz on conversational command structures.

#### **Session 9: Your Personal AI System (14:45 – 16:15)**

*   **Custom Instructions:** Set your default persona (Role, Tone, Format) so the AI always sounds like *you*, not a robot.
*   **The Workflow Library:** Build a personal "recipe book" (Notion or a spreadsheet) of your reusable workflows — the ones you built across the backlog.
    *   *Examples:* Meeting summaries, interview prep, difficult-conversation scripts.
*   **Habit Building:** Make reaching for a workflow the natural reflex for each bottleneck on your backlog.
*   **Build:** Package your best workflow into the library so a teammate could run it tomorrow.

#### **Session 10: The ROI Finale (16:30 – 17:30)**

*   **The Stack:** Deep Research → Structure → Design — the full pipeline, end to end.
*   **ROI Calculation:** Total your **Time Log**: **(Task Frequency × Duration × % AI Efficiency)** = hours per week won back.
*   **Showcase:** 3-minute presentation — "The one process I will never do manually again."
*   **Micro-Check:** Final Quiz on course ROI and key takeaways.
