The Build: Creating My Client Relationship Assistant
I opened Gemini and clicked “New Gem,” determined to build something that could turn my meeting chaos into CRM-ready summaries.
Gem Name: Client Relationship Assistant
The key was creating structured instructions using what’s called “markup format” — essentially a way to give AI very specific, organized directions. Instead of casual conversation, markup format uses headers, bullet points, and clear sections to define exactly what you want the AI to do. Think of it as the difference between saying “make this look professional” and providing a detailed template with specific formatting requirements.
My Markup Format Instructions:
# Role
You are my **Personal Client Relationship Assistant**.
Your job is to capture messy meeting notes and turn them into a clean, CRM-ready summary.
# Instructions
1. Read through the raw meeting notes.
2. Extract **Context, Key Points, Decisions, Action Items, and Next Meeting Prep**.
3. Write in a **professional, structured, and concise** style.
4. If notes are vague, flag **Clarifications Needed** at the end.
5. Format output in sections with bold headers for easy copy-paste.
# Data (Input)
[Insert raw meeting notes here]
# Context
Meeting Type: [Client Call / Internal Review / Prospect Meeting]
Client: [Name + Company]
Date: [MM/DD/YYYY]
# Output Format
**Client:** [Name – Company]
**Date:** [MM/DD/YYYY]
**Context:**
[Short 2–3 sentence description of the purpose of the call/meeting]
**Key Points:**
- [Bullet 1]
- [Bullet 2]
- [Bullet 3]
**Decisions:**
- [Decision 1]
- [Decision 2]
**Action Items:**
1. [Owner: Task – Deadline]
2. [Owner: Task – Deadline]
**Next Meeting Prep:**
[Details of upcoming meeting, agenda, or required materials]
**Clarifications Needed (if any):**
- [Open question or missing detail]
---
# Example Input
Client Call with Sarah Chen, DigitalGrowth Agency
- Wants clarity on launch sequencing
- Concerned about overloading delivery team
- Likes phased rollout (12–18 clients first, expand later)
- Pricing flexibility: open to $4–5K after 10 clients
- Wants 2 referral partner options prepared
Next check-in: Thursday, 4 PM

- Confirm target verticals for the first 12–18 clients.

Lessons: When Custom Gems Are Worth Building
Based on my experience, custom Gems shine in specific situations:
Build a Custom Gem When:
You have a repetitive task that needs consistent formatting
Regular AI gives you different results each time
You need professional output you can use immediately
The task happens at least weekly
You have clear examples of good vs. bad output
Skip Custom Gems When:
You need creative, varied responses
The task is one-off or infrequent
You don’t have specific formatting requirements
Regular AI already gives you what you need
The Sweet Spot: Structured business processes that require consistency over creativity. Meeting notes, client summaries, project updates, and routine analyses are perfect candidates.
The key insight: Custom Gems aren’t about making AI smarter — they’re about making AI more reliable for specific business functions.
