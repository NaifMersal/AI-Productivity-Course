# Session 2: The Workbench (Project Architecture)

**Duration:** 1.25 Hours
**Goal:** move "Context" from the Chat (Ephemeral) to the Project (Permanent).

---

## 10:45 - Part 1: The "Lazy Manager" Philosophy (15 min)

**Concept:** The Brain vs. The Conversation.
*   **Chat Window:** Short-term memory. Resets every time. Good for executing tasks.
*   **Project:** Long-term memory. Persists forever. Good for storing "Role" and "Context".

**Instructor Script:**
> "I don't want to type 'I am a Senior Analyst' every day. I want the AI to just KNOW it. We are going to build a 'Department' where the AI already knows the job."

---

## - Part 3: One-Sentence Delegation (15 min)

**The Payoff:**
*   **Instructor:** "Now that the Brain is built, look at my prompt."
    *   *Prompt:* "Create a learning plan for me."
*   **Result:** It generates a perfect plan *because it referenced the Project files*.
*   **Key Takeaway:** "We moved the complexity from the Prompt to the Architecture."
