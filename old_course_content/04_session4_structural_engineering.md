# Session 4: Structural Engineering (SOPs & Automation)

**Duration:** 1.75 Hours
**Goal:** Convert Manual Chains into an Automated SOP (Standard Operating Procedure).

---

## 14:45 - Part 1: The "Playbook" (10 min)

**Concept:** The SOP *is* the Manager.
**Instructor Script:**
> "In the last session, YOU were the glue holding the steps together. Now, we are going to write those steps down into a file, give it to the AI, and say 'Follow this map'."

**Demo:**
*   Show `skill_sop.md`.
*   Explain the structure:
    *   **Trigger:** When to run.
    *   **Steps:** Sequential instructions.
    *   **Output Format:** What the final result looks like.

![SOP Example](assets/session4_sop.png)

---

## 15:15 - Part 2: Automated Execution (45 min)

**The Turn:**
1.  **Upload the SOP:** specific `skill_sop.md` into the Project Knowledge.
2.  **The Magic Prompt:**
    *   *Prompt:* "Execute the Skill Acquisition Process for Ahmed."
3.  **Result:** Watch the AI execute Step 1, then Step 2, then Step 3... all automatically.

**Student Activity:**
1.  Draft a simple 3-step SOP for your process (Markdown format).
2.  Upload it to your Project.
3.  Run the "One-Sentence Delegation" again.

---

## 16:00 - Part 3: Peer Logic Check & Wrap (30 min)

**Activity:** "Break your Neighbor's SOP."
*   Swap seats/laptops.
*   Read their SOP.
*   Ask: "What happens if [Edge Case] occurs?"
    *   *Example:* "What if the invoice date is missing?"
*   **Homework:** Refine your SOP for tomorrow.
