Grading Rubric — Final Essay (ENG 210: Scientific Writing)

Total: 100 points

1. Thesis & Focus (20 pts)
   - Excellent (18-20): Clear, specific, arguable thesis sustained throughout
   - Good (14-17): Clear thesis with minor lapses in focus
   - Fair (10-13): Thesis is vague or inconsistently addressed
   - Poor (0-9): No identifiable thesis or argument

2. Evidence & Support (25 pts)
   - Excellent (22-25): Strong, relevant evidence from multiple credible sources; all claims supported
   - Good (18-21): Good evidence with minor gaps in support
   - Fair (13-17): Some claims unsupported; over-reliance on few sources
   - Poor (0-12): Insufficient or irrelevant evidence

3. Analysis & Critical Thinking (25 pts)
   - Excellent (22-25): Synthesizes sources, identifies patterns, offers original insight
   - Good (18-21): Good analysis but mostly descriptive rather than critical
   - Fair (13-17): Summarizes sources without meaningful synthesis
   - Poor (0-12): Merely reports or lists source content

4. Organization & Structure (15 pts)
   - Excellent (13-15): Logical flow with clear transitions; introduction and conclusion frame the argument
   - Good (10-12): Clear structure with minor organizational issues
   - Fair (7-9): Some organization but sections feel disconnected
   - Poor (0-6): Disorganized or incoherent structure

5. Style & Mechanics (15 pts)
   - Excellent (13-15): Clear, concise writing; error-free grammar; appropriate academic tone
   - Good (10-12): Mostly clear writing with minor errors
   - Fair (7-9): Frequent errors that impede clarity
   - Poor (0-6): Major grammatical issues; inappropriate tone

Note for graders: For each criterion, circle the score. Provide at least one specific comment per section. Final grade = sum of all sections.
