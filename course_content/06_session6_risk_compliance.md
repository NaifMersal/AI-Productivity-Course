## 11:45 - Part 2: Organizational Safety (30 min)

**Risk Handling:**
*   **Hallucinations:** The AI *will* lie if forced.
    *   *Fix:* Add "Refusal Instructions" to your SOP.
    *   *Example:* "If you cannot find the date in the text, DO NOT guess. State 'Date Not Finding'."

**Student Activity:**
*   Add a **"Verification Step"** to your SOP.
    *   *Example:* "Step 4: Verify that all extracted numbers match the source document."

---

## 12:15 - Part 3: The "Red Zones" (30 min)

**Discussion: When NOT to use AI.**
*   **The List:**
    1.  **Legal Contracts:** (Unless you are a lawyer using it for review).
    2.  **Hiring/Firing Decisions:** (Bias risk).
    3.  **Non-Public Financials:** (Unless using an Enterprise-tier private instance).
    4.  **Medical/Health Advice.**

*   **Exercise:** "Spot the Risk."
    *   Show 3 scenarios (e.g., "Uploading a patient record to check for typos").
    *   Class votes: Safe or Unsafe?
