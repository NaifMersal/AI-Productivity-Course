# Session 6: The Design Engine (09:00 – 10:45)

**Goal:** Speed & Polish. Turn your Session 5 "Blueprint" into a professional deck in minutes, not hours.

---

## 1. The Two Paths

We have the content (from Session 5). Now we need the pixels.
There are two ways to do this:
1.  **The "One-Click" Wonder:** Using AI-native tools (Gamma).
2.  **The "Corporate Safe" Way:** Using standard Office tools (Word -> PPT).

---

## 2. Method A: The "One-Click" Wonder (Gamma.app)

*Note: Requires internet access. Free tier is usually sufficient for demos.*

**The Concept:**
Tools like Gamma (or Tome/Beautiful.ai) are "Teslas." They drive themselves.

**The Workflow:**
1.  **Copy** your "Blueprint" (Outline) from Session 5.
2.  **Open** Gamma -> "Create New" -> "Paste Text".
3.  **Select** a theme (Professional, Dark, Vibrant).
4.  **Generate.**

**Pro-Tip:**
Use the "Edit with AI" chat in Gamma to refine slides.
> *"Make this image more corporate."*
> *"Turn this bullet list into a timeline."*

---

## 3. Method B: The "Corporate Safe" Way (Word to PowerPoint)

*Note: For when you MUST use the strict Company Template.*

**The Concept:**
PowerPoint has a hidden feature: It can design slides automatically from a **Word Outline**.

**The Workflow:**
1.  **Prompt:** Ask your AI (ChatGPT/Claude):
    > *"Convert this Slide Blueprint into a Markdown format that exports cleanly to a Word Outline. Use H1 for Slide Titles and H2 for Bullet points."*
2.  **Copy** the text into MS Word.
3.  **View:** Go to `View > Outline`. Ensure levels are correct (Level 1 = Title, Level 2 = Content).
4.  **Export:** `File > Export > Send to PowerPoint Presentation`.
5.  **Design:** In PowerPoint, click `Home > Designer` (The lightning bolt) to layout the slides instantly using your template.
