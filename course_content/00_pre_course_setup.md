**We operate with a "Safety First" mindset.**
1.  Gather 1-2 examples of documents used in your process (e.g., a past report, a policy PDF).
2.  **Sanitize them:**
    *   Replace real names with "Client A", "Technician X".
    *   Redact sensitive numbers (SSNs, specific financial totals if confidential).
    *   **Rule:** If you wouldn't leave it on a printer in a coffee shop, don't upload it raw.

## 2. Process Identification Worksheet

**Goal:** Pick the right battle. We want a process that is *high-value* but *low-risk* for your first build.

1.  **Process Name:** (e.g., Monthly Expense Reconciliation)
2.  **Trigger:** What starts this process? (e.g., "I receive email X", "It's the 1st of the month")
3.  **Inputs:** What data/documents do you need? (e.g., Excel export, PDF invoices)
4.  **Outputs:** What do you produce? (e.g., A summary email to the CFO)
5.  **Pain Point:** Why automate this? (e.g., "It takes 4 hours and is boring," "I make errors manually.")
