# Session 5: The Narrative Architect (The Amazon Method) (15:15 – 16:30)

**Goal:** Stop "Death by PowerPoint." Learn to build the *argument* before you build the *slides* using Chain of Thought (CoT).

---

## 1. The Problem: "Death by PowerPoint"

Most presentations fail not because the *design* is bad, but because the *story* is broken.
*   Too much text.
*   No clear argument.
*   "Frankenstein" decks pasted together from other files.

**The Fix:** The **Amazon Memo Method**.
Amazon banned PowerPoint in meetings. Instead, they write a **6-page Narrative Memo**. Why? Because writing forces you to think clearly.
We will use AI to do this thinking (Chain of Thought) for us.

---

## 2. The Technique: Chain of Thought (CoT)

**What is it?**
Asking the AI to "Think step-by-step" or "Show your work" before giving the answer.
This drastically reduces hallucinations and improves logic.

**The Workflow:**
1.  **The Brain Dump:** You give raw context (notes, goals).
2.  **The Narrative:** AI writes a 1-page memo (The Pitch).
3.  **The Roast:** AI debates/critiques the memo (The Stress Test).
4.  **The Blueprint:** AI converts the refined memo into a visual Slide Outline.

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: The Narrative Prompt
> **Role:** You represent the Project Lead.
> **Task:** Write a 1-page "Amazon Style" narrative memo pitching [Project X] to [Audience Y].
> **Context:**
> - Goal: Get $50k budget approved.
> - Problem: Current system is too slow.
> - Solution: Implement Tool Z.
> **Constraint:** Be concise, brutal, and data-driven. No fluff.

### Step 2: "The Roast" (Crucial Step)
Before we make slides, we must test our logic.
> **Role:** You are the skeptical CFO.
> **Task:** Roast this memo. Find the holes in the logic. Where is the ROI unclear? What risks are missing? Be harsh.

*(You then fix the holes based on the feedback)*

### Step 3: The Blueprint (Narrative to Slides)
Now we turn text into a visual plan.
> **Task:** Convert the approved Narrative into a 5-Slide Outline.
> **Format:**
> - **Slide Title:** (Action-oriented)
> - **The Hook:** (One sentence summary)
> - **Visual:** (Describe the EXACT chart or image needed usually. e.g., "Bar chart showing 20% growth", NOT "A picture of growth").
> - **Speaker Notes:** (Bullet points).

---

## 4. Student Activity: "The Narrative"

**Scenario:**
You are pitching a "Remote Work Policy" (or "New AI Tool") to your CEO, who is old-school and skeptical.

**Steps:**
1.  **Draft:** Use AI to write the 1-page argument.
2.  **Roast:** Ask AI to act as the "Skeptical CEO" and tear it apart.
3.  **Refine:** Ask AI to "Fix the logic gaps".
4.  **Blueprint:** Generate the 5-slide outline with **Visual Descriptors**.

**Why this matters:**
You are not just asking AI to "make slides." You are using it to **Think**.
