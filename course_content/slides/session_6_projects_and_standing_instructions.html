The “Groundhog Day” Problem

Stop repeating yourself

Yesterday You Wrote a Perfect Delegation

Your RICE prompt (Session 3) nailed the tone, the format, the rules.

It worked great.

Next Week…

New task. You open a fresh session. The agent has amnesia (Session 2).

  • It doesn’t know your role
  • It doesn’t know your tone or format rules
  • You type the Role and Context all over again

Productivity comes from reuse, not re-typing.

A Project = A Dedicated Office

One workspace per body of work

The Office, Per Project

A dedicated office (or folder) for one body of work, with its own filing cabinet of relevant documents — separate from your other work.

The app pulls that project’s pages onto the desk — and not everything else.

Why It Matters

“Q3 Board Report”

Its own files & rules

“Vendor Contracts”

Its own files & rules

Each gets the right background without you re-explaining — and they don’t bleed together.

The Wall Note

CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md

Standing Instructions

The note pinned to the office wall that the brain reads at the start of every job: who you are, your tone, your rules, your formats.

Two filenames, one idea

CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md are the same idea. Other apps call it custom instructions or a system prompt.

The Single Biggest Lever

Fix the tone, format, or a recurring rule here once — and it’s fixed for every future request.

No re-typing. No reminding. This is the highest-return five minutes in the course.
