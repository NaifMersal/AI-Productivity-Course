The Paradigm Shift

From “Search” to “Partner”

How Most People Use AI

Ask a question → Get an answer

This is the Search Mindset — and it wastes 90% of AI’s value.

The Partner Mindset

Give context → Iterate together → Get real results

Treat the AI like a smart intern who needs context, instruction, and feedback.

Example: Client Delay Email

Search Mindset:

“Write an email to a client about a delay.”

Result: Generic, robotic, sounds fake.

Example: Client Delay Email

Partner Mindset:

“I need to email Client X about a 3-day delay — we’re waiting for final internal approval. Tone: respectful but confident. Here is the project context…”

Result: Tailored, professional — sounds like you.

The 3 Traps

What limits new users

Trap 1: Generally treating it like Search

The Input Mistake

Asking short questions like a Google search.

The Fix: Better Inputs

Shift from Querying to Conversing. Give context.

Trap 2: Treating It Like a Person

The Interaction Mistake

“Please,” “Thank you,” “I’m sorry to bother you…”

The Fix: Direct Commands

Be direct. Clear commands > polite requests. Validate output, don’t compliment it.

Trap 3: Over-trusting

The Output Mistake

Assuming AI contains facts like a database.

Reality: AI is a Prediction Engine — it guesses the next word. It does not “know” facts.

How It Actually Works

The AI predicts the next word based on probability.

“The capital of Saudi Arabia is…”

  • Riyadh (99.8%) ✅
  • Jeddah (0.1%)
  • Paris (0.00001%)

It doesn’t “know” geography. It knows that “Riyadh” almost always follows that sentence.

Hold that thought

Why it predicts instead of knows — and what that means for everything you build — is the keystone of Session 2.

When It Guesses Wrong (Hallucinations)

What happens when it doesn’t know the answer? It guesses.

“According to Article 214 of the new Saudi Investment Law: …”

  • The (45%)
  • Foreign (30%)
  • Capital (15%)

The AI constructs a sentence that sounds legal, but it has never read the actual document.

Trust but Verify

The Rule

AI is a drafting tool, not a fact-checking tool.

Trust AI for:

  • Logic & structure
  • Drafting & writing
  • Brainstorming

Verify AI on:

  • Facts & dates
  • Math & calculations
  • Legal citations

Where AI Pays Off

Pick the right work to hand over

The Bottleneck Test

Hand a task to AI when it is:

✅ Good fit

  • Repetitive & mundane — routine emails, status updates
  • Data-intensive — summarizing, finding patterns
  • Drafting & brainstorming — beating the blank page
  • Predictable — clear, logical steps

⚠️ Keep a human in the loop

  • High-stakes — ethics, safety, big consequences
  • Deeply human — empathy, relationships
  • Confidential — sensitive or proprietary data
  • Originality is the point — a truly unique take

Safety First

The “Newspaper Test”

The Newspaper Test

Before pasting anything into AI, ask:

“If this appeared on the front page of the New York Times, would I be fired or sued?”

Yes or Maybe → DO NOT PASTE IT.

High-Risk Data

Never share with AI

  • PII — Names, National IDs (Iqama/Saudi ID), phone numbers
  • Financial secrets — Unreleased earnings, bank accounts, salary data
  • Trade secrets — Proprietary code, formulas, unreleased strategy docs

Data Hygiene

The Art of Sanitization

Sanitization: 3 Steps

Remove the specifics, keep the structure.

  1. Anonymize — “Mohammed Al-Salem” → “[Employee A]”
  2. Generalize — “5,000,000 SAR” → “5M+ SAR”
  3. Strip identifiers — Remove company names, project codes, addresses

Before: Unsafe

“Layla Al-Harbi missed her Q3 sales target of 1.5M SAR by 15%. She blamed the delay in the Riyadh Season Campaign launch.”

Names, amounts, projects — all exposed.

After: Sanitized

“[Employee] missed their quarterly sales target by 15%. They cited delays in [Key Project] launch as the primary reason.”

Now you can safely ask: “Draft constructive feedback for [Employee] based on this…”

Micro-Check

Safe or Risky?

Quiz — Round 1

1. “Summarize the key risks in these meeting notes: [internal minutes about unannounced merger targets]”

RISKY — Material Non-Public Information (MNPI)

2. “I have a budget of 375,000 SAR for a National Day campaign. Suggest a media mix.”

SAFE — General numbers, no sensitive context

Quiz — Round 2

3. “Summarize this PDF: [Q3 Investor Presentation from STC — publicly released]”

SAFE — Publicly available information

4. “Draft a termination letter for Employee #4421 who missed targets. No name used.”

SAFE — PII removed, only behavior described

Quiz — Round 3

5. “Analyze 5,000 customer records (Names, IBANs, Transaction IDs) to find spending patterns.”

RISKY — Massive PII leak. Never upload raw customer databases.

Key Takeaways

  1. Partner, not search engine — give context, iterate
  2. Avoid the 3 traps — Search, Person, Over-Trust
  3. Automate the right work — repetitive, data-heavy, drafting, predictable
  4. Trust but verify — draft with AI, fact-check yourself
  5. Newspaper Test & sanitize — if in doubt, don’t paste it
  6. Your backlog is the target — we automate it over the next three days
