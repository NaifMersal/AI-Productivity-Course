# Sample Source for Gemini Notebook Practice

If you don't have a report or policy document handy, use one of these openly accessible
resources:

## Option 1 — Public research report (open access)
**Title:** "The State of Remote Work" — an openly published annual survey report
**Where:** Search for a recent open-access "state of remote work" or "future of work" report
(many consultancies publish the full PDF for free). Download the PDF and upload it to
Gemini Notebook.

## Option 2 — Saudi government policy document
**Title:** Saudi Vision 2030 — Overview Document
**URL:** https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/
Open the Vision 2030 site and download the official overview PDF, then upload it to
Gemini Notebook. This is a public-domain government document.

## Option 3 — Use your own
Any report, policy PDF, or briefing document you are already working with. Remember the
**Newspaper Test** — don't upload confidential customer data or unreleased plans.

---

*Pro tip: Gemini Notebook also accepts Google Docs, web links, and pasted text. You can paste the
content directly if you can't download the PDF.*
