Review Checklist — Internal Report / Recommendation Memo

Total: 100 points

1. Clear Recommendation (20 pts)
   - Excellent (18-20): States one specific, actionable recommendation up front and sustains it
   - Good (14-17): Clear recommendation with minor drift
   - Fair (10-13): Recommendation is vague or buried
   - Poor (0-9): No identifiable recommendation

2. Evidence & Data (25 pts)
   - Excellent (22-25): Every claim backed by data or a named source; numbers are specific
   - Good (18-21): Mostly supported, minor gaps
   - Fair (13-17): Several claims unsupported; relies on "some people say"
   - Poor (0-12): Little or no evidence; anecdotal only

3. Analysis & Insight (25 pts)
   - Excellent (22-25): Diagnoses root causes, weighs trade-offs, offers a non-obvious insight
   - Good (18-21): Solid analysis but mostly descriptive
   - Fair (13-17): Summarizes the situation without real analysis
   - Poor (0-12): Merely restates the problem

4. Structure & Flow (15 pts)
   - Excellent (13-15): Logical flow; clear intro and conclusion frame the recommendation
   - Good (10-12): Clear structure with minor issues
   - Fair (7-9): Sections feel disconnected
   - Poor (0-6): Disorganized

5. Clarity & Tone (15 pts)
   - Excellent (13-15): Concise, professional, decision-ready; free of filler
   - Good (10-12): Mostly clear, minor wordiness
   - Fair (7-9): Wordy or vague; hard to skim
   - Poor (0-6): Unclear or unprofessional tone

Note for reviewers: For each criterion, note the score and give at least one specific comment.
Final score = sum of all sections.
