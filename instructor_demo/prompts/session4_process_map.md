# Session 4: SOP Installation Demo

**Duration:** 30 min demo + 30 min student install + 15 min wrap

---

## The Problem Session 3 Exposed
> "YOU were the glue holding the chain together. If you forgot Step 1 by Step 4, it broke."

**The Solution:** Write the chain as an SOP and give it to the AI.

---

## Demo: Installing the SOP

1. **Show `skill_sop.md`** - "This is the 4-step chain we just did manually, written as instructions."
2. **Upload to Project** - The AI now has the full workflow.

---

## The Payoff: One-Sentence Delegation

### Prompt:
```
Based on skill_sop and personal profile, I want to learn Python for automating my month-end close process in 8 weeks with 3 hours/week.
```

### What Happens:
The SOP defines the chain (Step 1 → 2 → 3 → 4). The AI executes all steps automatically.

**Teaching Point:**
> "In Session 3, YOU were the glue. In Session 4, THE SOP is the glue. You moved yourself out of the loop."

---

Upload your SOP and test the "full chain" prompt. Does it execute all steps?
